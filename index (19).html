The guy with the list

More than a decade on stages all over Michigan.

Paul Pipitone is a metro Detroit comedian, host, and producer. His act comes out of life in an Italian family and a lifelong love of Goodfellas and the Rocky movies.

Every Monday he runs the list at New Way Bar in Ferndale, the open mic he produces and hosts, and the one his bio calls his longtime, legendary room. One review counted 60 or 70 comics coming through in a single night.

He has been a special guest on Darren McCarty's Slapstick Comedy Tour, worked Comedy 4 Cancer benefits in Warren, and talked comedy, coffee, and the Lions on podcasts. For at least one comic, he was the first host to ever bring her to the stage.
